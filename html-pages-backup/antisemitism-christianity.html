INSTITUTIONAL DISCRIMINATION

The Dhimmi System: Institutionalized Oppression

📜 Pact of Umar (7th-9th Century) - Rules for Christians/Jews:

  • ✗ Cannot build new churches, synagogues, or monasteries
  • ✗ Cannot repair existing ones without permission
  • ✗ Must wear distinctive clothing (yellow badge for Jews, blue for Christians)
  • ✗ Cannot display crosses or religious symbols publicly
  • ✗ Cannot ring church bells or pray loudly
  • ✗ Must stand when Muslims enter room
  • ✗ Cannot ride horses (only donkeys/mules)
  • ✗ Cannot carry weapons
  • ✗ Testimony in court worth half that of Muslim
  • ✗ Must pay jizya tax (often 50%+ of income)
  • ✗ Homes must be lower than Muslim homes
  • ✗ Cannot hold positions of authority over Muslims
  • ✗ Cannot proselytize or prevent family members from converting to Islam
  • ✗ Face public humiliation during jizya payment ceremony
"Fight those who do not believe in Allah... until they give the jizyah willingly while they are humbled." Quran 9:29

💰 Jizya Tax System:

The jizya was not merely a tax - it was a systematic humiliation designed to pressure conversion:

  • Often 50-80% of income
  • Public humiliation during payment (struck on neck, forced to stand while Muslim sits)
  • Failure to pay = enslavement, execution, or forced conversion
  • Women, children sometimes enslaved if men couldn't pay
  • Created economic incentive to convert (Muslims exempt from jizya)

Result: Over centuries, economic pressure forced gradual conversion of entire populations.
