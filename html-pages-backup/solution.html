🕊️ Our Position: Freedom of/from Religion, But Truth First

The Ex-Muslim community is NOT calling for banning Islam or stopping people from practicing their faith.

We believe in freedom of belief and freedom FROM belief. People have the right to practice any religion - but NOT the right to impose it on others, harm others, or indoctrinate children with violent, hateful ideologies.

Our message is direct: If you want to keep believing, that's your right. But you must:

  • ✋ STOP LYING ABOUT THE TEXTS - Admit what the Quran and Hadith actually say
  • ✋ STOP TEACHING HATE TO CHILDREN - Don't pass violence and supremacism to next generation
  • ✋ ADMIT THE QURAN HAS MORAL PROBLEMS - It's not perfect; it contains violence, misogyny, slavery
  • ✋ EMBRACE UNIVERSAL HUMAN RIGHTS - Modern values over 7th-century tribal law

We reject the "Islam has good parts too" narrative. Islamic "charity" is for Muslims only. Islamic "community" excludes and opposes non-believers. Islamic "morality" is tribal, not universal. The entire foundation is supremacist - there's no "keeping the good" when the ideology itself divides humanity into believers vs kuffar.

❌ What We Are NOT Saying

We Are NOT Asking To:

  • Ban Islam: People have the right to believe what they choose
  • Force Atheism: We respect people's need for spirituality and community
  • Discriminate Against Muslims: Individual Muslims are not the problem - the texts and ideologies are
  • Promote Hatred: We oppose hate from all sources, including Islamophobia
  • Remove All Religion: Religious communities provide value when focused on peace, charity, and moral growth
  • Blame All Muslims: Millions of Muslims are peaceful, kind, and reject violence - they deserve support in reforming their faith

🎯 Our Core Principle:

Ideas can be criticized. Books can be reformed. Ideologies can be challenged.

This is not about attacking people - it's about protecting humanity from harmful doctrines that inspire violence, oppression, and suffering.

TEXT REFORM EDUCATION

The Problem: Violent verses in Quran and Hadith are taught as eternal, perfect, and applicable today.

  • Remove from children's curriculum: Verses about killing unbelievers, wife-beating, slavery, sex slaves - ban from being taught to children
  • Islamic scholars issue unified fatwa: Declare verses commanding violence as "abrogated by modern human rights"
  • Reinterpret as "spiritual struggle": Reframe jihad as internal moral struggle, not physical warfare

Verses That MUST Be Reformed/Contextualized:

2. Stop Indoctrinating Children - Protect the Next Generation

EDUCATION CHILD PROTECTION

The Problem: Children are taught from age 3-4 that Quran is perfect, Muhammad is perfect, non-Muslims are inferior, and violent verses are Allah's eternal commands.

The Solution:

  • Ban teaching violent verses to minors: International law should prohibit teaching hate speech to children under 18
  • Reform madrasa curriculum: Focus on ethics, morality, critical thinking - NOT memorizing violent verses
  • Teach comparative religion: Show children multiple faiths; develop critical thinking; prevent indoctrination
  • Require consent for religious education: Children should reach age of reason (16-18) before religious instruction
  • Monitor extremist content: Shut down madrasas teaching jihad, hatred of non-Muslims, martyrdom glorification
  • Parental accountability: Parents who teach children to hate should face intervention, not celebration

MODERN VALUES HUMAN RIGHTS

The Problem: Many Muslim-majority countries prioritize Sharia over human rights, resulting in oppression, inequality, and violence.
